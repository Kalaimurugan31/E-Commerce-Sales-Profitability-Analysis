E-Commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to understand business performance, profitability, customer behaviour, discount impact, shipping performance and product returns.

The goal is to identify important business trends, understand factors affecting profitability and provide data-driven business recommendations.

Project Objectives

* Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify the best-performing product categories and sub-categories.
* Compare sales and profitability across regions and customer segments.
* Understand the relationship between discounts and profit.
* Analyse monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Evaluate product return rates.
* Identify loss-making products and areas for improvement.

Tools & Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* MySQL
* Power BI
* Microsoft Excel

Dataset

The project uses the Sample Superstore dataset, containing order, customer, product, sales, discount, profit and shipping information.

Additional returns data was used to analyse returned orders.

Project Workflow

1. Data Cleaning – Python

* Imported the dataset using Pandas.
* Checked missing values and duplicate records.
* Converted date columns into appropriate formats.
* Identified and excluded future-dated records from historical performance analysis.

2. Exploratory Data Analysis – Python

* Analysed sales and profit distributions.
* Compared product categories and customer segments.
* Examined discount patterns.
* Created visualisations using Matplotlib and Seaborn.

3. SQL Analysis – MySQL

Performed business analysis using SQL queries:

* Overall business performance.
* Category-wise and region-wise profitability.
* Monthly and yearly sales trends.
* Sub-category profitability.
* Discount impact on profit.
* Top and bottom profitable customers.
* Loss-making products.
* Shipping performance.
* Product return analysis.

4. Dashboard Development – Power BI

Created an interactive dashboard containing:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Profit Margin

Added interactive Year, Region and Category slicers to explore business performance.

Key Business Insights

1. Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the three product categories.
2. The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
3. Consumer customers contributed the largest share of sales.
4. Tables, Bookcases and Supplies were loss-making sub-categories.
5. Higher discount levels were generally associated with lower profit margins.
6. Copiers generated the highest total profit among sub-categories.
7. Technology recorded the highest return rate among the three categories.
8. Standard Class was the most frequently used shipping method.

Business Recommendations

* Review pricing and discount strategies for loss-making products.
* Investigate the reasons behind higher returns in the West region.
* Focus on profitable product categories and sub-categories.
* Monitor product-level profitability regularly.
* Improve business decisions using interactive sales and profitability reports.
